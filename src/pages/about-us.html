Six Decades of Beverage Heritage

Founded in Singapore in 1994, IncoBev Asia is a leading integrated beverage solutions company specialising in commercial beverage systems, coffee, HoReCa, and regional beverage distribution across Southeast Asia.

60+ Years of Heritage

Our Evolution

Backed by over six decades of regional operating heritage through its parent group, IncoBev Asia has evolved from one of Southeast Asia’s leading consumer coffee businesses into a diversified regional beverage solutions group.

Today we serve businesses across hospitality, foodservice, workplace, retail, and institutional sectors — combining equipment, consumables, manufacturing, technical expertise, and customer success capabilities. The origins of the Group trace back to 1963, when the business first began operating within Southeast Asia’s consumer beverage industry.

How We Grew — 60 Years of Growth & Partnership

history
1963 – 1991

Origins

Group begins operations in Southeast Asia (1963). Suzuki Cafe brings the first Japanese gourmet coffee to Singapore; appointed Franke distributor; Suzuki Coffee Roastery established.

foundation
1994 – 2012

Group Foundation

Fieber Tradings founded in Singapore (1994). Suzuki joins the Group and scales its Chin Bee roastery; expansion into Malaysia; Kaffa Kaldi, Schaerer, and Sun Siam Thailand join.

workspace_premium
2017 – 2022

Premium Expansion

Entered Indonesia via PT Ambrosius Utama. Appointed San Remo, La San Marco, Zummo and La Marzocco; became official licensee for Robert Timms Professional Coffee.

2024 – 2026

IncoBev Asia

Launched a dedicated HORECA Division in Thailand, then restructured Fieber Tradings into IncoBev Asia — the regional holding company overseeing all brands and business units across SEA.
