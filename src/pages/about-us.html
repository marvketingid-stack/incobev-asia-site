Our Journey

60 years of growth & partnership — select an era to explore that chapter.

1963 – 1991

Origins

The Group began operating within Southeast Asia’s consumer beverage industry in 1963. Suzuki Cafe brought the first Japanese gourmet coffee to Singapore (Bukit Timah), IncoBev was appointed Franke distributor, and the Suzuki Coffee Roastery was established in Ang Mo Kio.

1994 – 2012

Group Foundation

Fieber Tradings was founded in Singapore (1994). Suzuki joined the Group and scaled its Chin Bee roastery, operations expanded into Malaysia, and Kaffa Kaldi, Schaerer and Sun Siam Thailand joined IncoBev Asia.

2017 – 2022

Premium Expansion

Entered Indonesia via PT Ambrosius Utama. Appointed San Remo, La San Marco, Zummo and La Marzocco, and became the official licensee for Robert Timms Professional Coffee.

2024 – 2026

IncoBev Asia

Launched a dedicated HORECA Division in Thailand, then restructured Fieber Tradings into IncoBev Asia — the regional holding company consolidating and overseeing all brand portfolios, business units and strategic investments across Southeast Asia.
