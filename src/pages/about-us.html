About Us

Founded in Singapore in 1994, IncoBev Asia is a leading integrated beverage solutions company specialising in commercial beverage systems, coffee, HoReCa, and regional beverage distribution across Southeast Asia.

60+ Years of Heritage

Who We Are

Backed by over six decades of regional operating heritage through its parent group, IncoBev Asia has evolved from one of Southeast Asia’s leading consumer coffee businesses into a diversified regional beverage solutions group.

Today we serve businesses across hospitality, foodservice, workplace, retail, and institutional sectors — combining equipment, consumables, manufacturing, technical expertise, and customer success capabilities. The origins of the Group trace back to 1963, when the business first began operating within Southeast Asia’s consumer beverage industry.

Our Journey

60 years of growth & partnership — select a year to explore that chapter.

1963The Beginning

Group begins operations in Southeast Asia

The origins of the Group trace back to 1963, when the business first began operating within Southeast Asia’s consumer beverage industry.

1979Suzuki Cafe

Suzuki Cafe established

Introduced the first Japanese gourmet coffee to Singapore, with the first shop established in Bukit Timah, Singapore.

1991Roastery

Suzuki Coffee Roastery established

As demand for high-quality coffee grew, Suzuki expanded its operations and introduced a roastery in Ang Mo Kio, Singapore.

1994Foundation

Fieber Tradings founded in Singapore

At a time when premium coffee solutions and advanced beverage equipment were still uncommon, Fieber Tradings was established to help businesses elevate their beverage programmes.

2005Malaysia

Expansion into Malaysia

Suzuki Coffee expanded operations into Malaysia to meet the rising demand for gourmet coffee across Southeast Asia and growing tourism.

2012Thailand

Sun Siam Thailand joins the Group

Specialising in the distribution of ready-to-drink beverages and brands, Sun Siam Thailand joined IncoBev Asia Group.

2017Indonesia

Entered the Indonesian market

Expanded operations into Indonesia through PT Ambrosius Utama to capture the growing HORECA opportunity.

2022Premium Brands

Premium brand portfolio expansion

Appointed Zummo and La Marzocco distributor — bringing premium espresso equipment and fresh juice extraction systems to the region.

2026IncoBev Asia

Restructured into IncoBev Asia

Fieber Tradings was restructured into IncoBev Asia — the regional holding company consolidating and overseeing all brand portfolios, business units and strategic investments across Southeast Asia.
