About Us

Who We Are

Backed by over six decades of regional operating heritage through its parent group, IncoBev Asia has evolved from one of Southeast Asia’s leading consumer coffee businesses into a diversified regional beverage solutions group.

Today we serve businesses across hospitality, foodservice, workplace, retail, and institutional sectors — combining equipment, consumables, manufacturing, technical expertise, and customer success capabilities.

Our Journey

Six decades of growth & partnership — pick a period, then move through the years within it.

1963 – 1991

From Consumer Coffee to Coffee Expertise

1963

Group begins operations in Southeast Asia

The origins of the Group trace back to 1963, when the business first began operating within Southeast Asia’s consumer beverage industry.

1979

Established Suzuki Cafe

Introduced the first Japanese gourmet coffee to Singapore, with the first shop established in Bukit Timah, Singapore.

1981

Established Suzuki Coffee Roastery

As demand for high-quality coffee experiences increased, Suzuki expanded its operations and introduced a roastery in Ang Mo Kio, Singapore.

1991

Appointed Franke distributor in Singapore

As hospitality standards evolved, the business identified an opportunity to transform how beverages were served within HoReCa environments across Southeast Asia.

1994 – 2019

Building a Regional Beverage Group

1994

Founded FT Holdings in Singapore

At a time when premium coffee solutions and advanced beverage equipment were still relatively uncommon, Fieber Tradings was established to help businesses elevate their beverage programs.

1995

Suzuki joins IncoBev Asia Group and expanded operations

Suzuki Coffee moved into its permanent 55,000 sq. ft. roastery at Chin Bee Avenue, enabling larger-scale production and reinforcing its position as a market leader.

2005

Expanded operations into Malaysia

Suzuki Coffee continues to expand operations to meet rising demand of gourmet coffee across Southeast Asia growing tourism.

2008

Kaffa Kaldi Singapore joins IncoBev Asia Group

Leading premier supplier of comprehensive beverage solutions in Singapore joins IncoBev Asia Group.

2010

Appointed Schaerer distributor in Singapore

Technical excellence in Swiss-engineered commercial coffee equipment.

2017

Expanded operations into Indonesia through PT Ambrosius Utama (PMA)

Entered Indonesian market to capture growing HORECA opportunity.

2019

Restructure of IncoBev Asia

Restructuring Fieber Tradings into IncoBev Asia as the regional holding company, consolidating and overseeing all brand portfolios, business units, and strategic investments across SEA.

2021 – 2026

Strengthening Our Regional Platform

2021

Appointed La San Marco Distributor; Official licensee for Robert Timms Professional Coffee

Strengthening our coffee ecosystem by roasting Australian coffee brands in our Singapore headquarters for the Southeast Asian market.

2022

Appointed Zummo and La Marzocco distributor

Premium espresso equipment and fresh juice extraction systems.

2024

Appointed San Remo distributor

Italian excellence in commercial espresso machines.

2025

Expanded HORECA Division in Thailand under IncoBev Thailand

Launched dedicated HORECA Division in Thailand to focus exclusively on hotels and work spaces.

2026

Appointed Nuova Simonelli distributor

Innovative professional espresso machines that combine advanced coffee technology with intuitive, user-friendly design.

Continued Excellence

Over the decades, the company expanded both organically and through strategic acquisitions and partnerships, building a strong regional ecosystem.

Today, IncoBev Asia serves as a trusted regional beverage partner supporting businesses across multiple industries throughout Southeast Asia.
