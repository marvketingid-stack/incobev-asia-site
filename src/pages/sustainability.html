Our Sustainability Approach

Building a Sustainable Future Together

Our commitment to environmental responsibility runs across the entire supply chain — guided by five interconnected pillars and a clear path to net-zero by 2030.

Our Climate Commitment

We are working toward net-zero carbon emissions across our operations by 2030 — partnering with suppliers and customers to decarbonise the beverage value chain across Southeast Asia.

Updated: Q1 2026

The 5 Sustainability Pillars

A multi-faceted approach to regional impact — from how we source and operate to the communities and customers we serve.

eco

Responsible Sourcing

Building direct relationships with coffee producers and cooperatives for fair prices, quality, and sustainable farming.

  • • Direct-from-farm & collective sourcing
  • • Rainforest Alliance certified farms
  • • Purpose-aligned, ethical traders
factory

Responsible Operations

Efficient, FSSC 22000-certified manufacturing powered by renewable solar energy, with a culture of operational excellence.

  • • FSSC 22000-certified facility
  • • Solar-powered roasting
  • • Reduced water use & waste minimisation
  • • Lean, continuous-improvement culture
recycling

Circular Resource Management

Designing waste out of the system through recyclable packaging, machine refurbishment, and coffee-grounds upcycling.

  • • Recyclable / compostable packaging (target Q4 2026)
  • • Machine recovery & refurbishment
  • • Coffee-grounds upcycling
  • • Right-sized packaging & material reduction
groups

People & Communities

Guided by the SuzukiCARES framework, putting people at the heart of everything we do.

  • • Community outreach
  • • Associate growth
  • • Regional suppliers
  • • Engaging consumers
handshake

Shared Value Creation

Helping our customers cut their own Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions across their beverage operations.

  • • Energy-efficient machines & monitoring
  • • Green delivery solutions
  • • Coffee recycling programme
  • • Trade-in & responsible disposal
