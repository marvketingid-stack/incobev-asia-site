IncoBev Asia was pleased to participate in SIGEP Asia 2026, bringing together professionals, businesses and industry players from across the coffee, foodservice and hospitality sectors.

As a regional beverage solutions group, industry events such as SIGEP Asia provide an important platform for us to connect with the market, exchange ideas and gain insights into the evolving needs of businesses across the region.

## Connecting with the Industry

SIGEP Asia brings together a diverse community of professionals across coffee, bakery, foodservice and hospitality. The event provided an opportunity for IncoBev Asia to engage with industry professionals, customers and business partners while gaining a broader understanding of emerging trends and market developments.

These conversations help us better understand the challenges businesses face and the opportunities shaping the future of the beverage industry.

## Strengthening Our Partnership with Franke

Participation in industry events is also an important part of building strong relationships across the beverage ecosystem.

From customers and business partners to industry professionals and potential collaborators, SIGEP Asia created opportunities to exchange knowledge, explore new possibilities and strengthen connections across the region.

## Looking Ahead

As the beverage industry continues to evolve, staying connected to the market remains essential. Events such as SIGEP Asia allow us to stay close to industry developments, understand changing business needs and identify opportunities for collaboration.

This supports our broader vision to elevate Asia's beverage experience through world-class brands that inspire excellence.

As we continue to grow across the region, IncoBev Asia remains committed to building strong partnerships, bringing together industry expertise and capabilities, and contributing to the continued development of Asia's beverage landscape.
